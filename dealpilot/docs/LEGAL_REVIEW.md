# Items requiring specialist legal and regulatory review before public launch

DealPilot is designed conservatively, but the following need advice from an Australian lawyer (and,
where noted, a compliance specialist) before real customers use it. This is a checklist, not advice.

## Consumer law (Competition and Consumer Act / Australian Consumer Law)
1. **Savings claims.** Realised vs projected savings wording, "Never overpay again" tagline, and any
   marketing using savings figures — misleading or deceptive conduct risk.
2. **Fee disclosure.** The AUD $1.50 success fee: when and how it's disclosed (before acceptance), the
   "qualifying transaction" definition, the cap at the saving, refunds and disputes.
3. **Unfair contract terms** in customer and business terms of use (the 2023 penalties regime).
4. **Comparison and ranking neutrality.** How offers are ranked; disclosure if commissions are ever
   introduced (ACCC digital platforms guidance). Rankings currently ignore any payment to DealPilot.
5. **Reviews.** Verified-job-only reviews; moderation and removal policy (ACCC online reviews guidance).

## AI agents and negotiation
6. **Agency and authority.** Whether DealPilot acts as the customer's agent when sending messages in
   Approval or Limited Autopilot mode; scope of the authority granted; liability for AI-drafted messages.
7. **No contracts formed by AI.** Confirm the final-terms approval flow, wording, and that DealPilot is not
   a party to customer–business contracts.
8. **Automated decision-making transparency** obligations under the Privacy Act reforms.

## Privacy (Privacy Act 1988 / APPs)
9. Privacy policy and collection notices; overseas disclosure (APP 8) if the AI provider or email
   provider processes data offshore; consent wording for AI processing.
10. Document retention periods; anonymisation approach on deletion; record-keeping obligations.
11. Notifiable Data Breaches readiness.

## Sector-specific
12. **Energy.** Comparator conduct rules and the use of Energy Made Easy / CDR Product Reference Data
    (attribution, accuracy, "estimate" wording); whether helping customers switch triggers any
    retail/broker authorisation; state rules (e.g. Victorian Energy Compare).
13. **Insurance and finance.** The insurance category must stay general information. Confirm no
    financial product advice or dealing (AFSL) is involved, including in AI-drafted messages.
14. **Trades licensing.** Wording of "verified" for licences checked by an administrator; liability if
    a licence is later found invalid; jurisdiction-specific display requirements.
15. **Telco.** Any switching assistance and the Telecommunications Consumer Protections Code.

## Payments and tax (before enabling charges)
16. Payment terms, tax invoices and GST on the success fee; whether holding or routing any funds
    creates payments-licensing obligations (DealPilot is designed never to hold customer funds).
17. Consumer Data Right accreditation if read-only bank data is added to Money Guardian.

## Corporate
18. Business user terms: verification, suspension and appeal process; acceptable use.
19. Group buying: confirm no commitments, deposits or pooled funds (none in Phase 2).
